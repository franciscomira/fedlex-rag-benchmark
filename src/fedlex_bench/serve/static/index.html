Fedlex cross-lingual search

Ask a question about Swiss federal law in English, German, French or Italian. The demo returns the most relevant articles of the statute, with the German, French and Italian texts side by side. It does not write answers.

Important notices

{{NOTICES}}

How good is it? The ranking comes from a small multilingual embedding model that was fine-tuned for this corpus. It is wrong sometimes: the article you need may be ranked low or may be missing from the top results, and a high score does not mean the article answers your question. The score is a similarity between your question and the best-matching passage, not a probability. Always read the article itself and check the Fedlex source.

Privacy. This app does not store or log your question. It is sent to the server as a request body (not in the address bar) and used only to find articles. The hosting platform may keep ordinary connection logs.
